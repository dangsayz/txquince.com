# Content clusters: built from what families actually complain about

Research date: 2026-10-31. The live cluster map is `src/content/blog-clusters.ts`; every post in a cluster shows its whole series with "read next", so readers keep going and every page links to its pillar and siblings.

## Where the venting is, and how we use it

| Source | What it gives us | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Local news (Univision, Telemundo, KRGV, KENS 5, NBC/ABC "Responds" segments) | Documented cases with dollar amounts, timelines and outcomes | Cite and link directly. This is the backbone of the "Protect your money" pillar. |
| Facebook quince groups (DFW buy/sell/recommend groups) | The rawest, most local complaints | Private: never quote, screenshot or name anyone. Read the threads, note the *pattern* ("photographer went silent after the party"), and answer the pattern in an article. |
| Reddit, WeddingWire forums | Regret stories ("wish we had video", "friend with a camera") | Paraphrase themes only. Most results are wedding threads; use them as supporting color, not quince facts. |
| Your own DMs, inquiries and plan leads | Exact words DFW moms use | The best source. Every question asked twice becomes a spoke. |

Ethics: a family's bad day is not marketing copy. We cite public reporting, never private posts, and never shame a named vendor beyond what a news report itself states.

## What families are worried about (pain points)

1. **Paid in full, never received photos or video.** Dallas: more than $1,600, still waiting after six months ([Univision 23](https://www.univision.com/local/dallas-kuvn/localisimo/quinceanera-las-fotos-y-videos-por-las-que-pago-mas-de-1600-dolares)). RGV: more than $2,000, nothing after almost a year ([KRGV](https://www.krgv.com/news/empresa-del-valle-incumple-con-entrega-de-fotos-a-quincea-era/)). Laredo: an arrest ([PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/05/27/photographer-arrested-for-allegedly-failing-to-deliver-quinceanera-photos/)).
2. **Late and poor quality.** San Diego: photos after about nine months, not professionally edited, no video ([NBC 7](https://www.nbcsandiego.com/nbc-7-responds-2/san-diego-quinceanera-photos-fall-short/3998184/)). The Telemundo version's headline says she'd have been better off with a phone ([Telemundo 20](https://www.telemundo20.com/responde/madre-san-diego-decepcionadas-con-fotos-quinceanera-mejor-tomaba-celular/2511646/)).
3. **Bundles and middlemen.** A limo package that never paid the photographer ([ABC7](https://abc7news.com/post/sf-family-hires-quinceanera-photographer-never-gets-photos/5695017/)). A planner charged with grand theft ([NBC Bay Area](https://www.nbcbayarea.com/news/local/san-jose-event-planner-quinceanera-scam/3957744/)).
4. **Venues.** A San Antonio hall accused of taking deposits, double-booking and going silent ([KENS 5](https://www.kens5.com/article/features/producers-picks/customers-claim-san-antonio-event-center-has-taken-thousands-of-dollars-from-families/273-620619829)).
5. **Dresses.** Late twin dresses a week before the party ([KRGV](https://www.krgv.com/videos/mother-devastated-after-shop-refuses-to-refund-late-quinceanera-dresses)). A Phoenix boutique sued after taking about $44,000 in deposits ([KJZZ](https://www.kjzz.org/2024-03-07/content-1873487-arizona-ag-says-quinceanera-dress-shop-took-44k-deposits-then-suddenly-closed)).
6. **Impersonators.** Fake accounts charging for Bobby Pulido appearances ([Tejano Nation](https://tejanonation.net/2026/05/21/bobby-pulido-warns-fans-about-quinceanera-scam-accounts-charging-for-appearances/)).
7. **The court falls apart.** Chambelanes drop out, often within a month of the date; breakups are the usual cause ([court guide](https://www.newdawnphoto.com/blog/quinceanera-court-damas-chambelanes)).
8. **Regret from going cheap.** "A friend with a camera", "only 8 usable photos", "wish we had video" (wedding forums; supporting color only).
9. **Booking too late.** Specialists say 6 to 12 months; August to October and Saturdays fill first.

## Linking rules (so the clusters compound)
1. Every spoke links to its pillar in the first third of the article, and the pillar links to every spoke.
2. The series block (automatic) handles sibling links. Add 1–2 bridges in the prose to another cluster where it's natural ("deposits" ↔ "budget").
3. Every article ends with the `/plan` CTA with its own `src` tag (e.g. `?src=blog-vendors`), so `/admin/plans` shows which articles produce leads.
4. Write EN and ES together and add the pair to `EN_ES_PAIRS` in `src/content/blog.ts`; the Spanish series appears automatically.
5. A new spoke = one question, answered in the first paragraph, with one concrete number.

## Owner facts (answered 2026-10-31)
1. Delivery: 30 to 90 days after the party, depending on the season.
2. Backup: if anyone on the team can't make it, one of the two owners covers the day, no exceptions.
3. Sharing: the agreement includes permission to share photos publicly. Note: the agreement the site generates (`src/lib/agreement.ts`) lets a family opt out at signing ("We will not share your photos or videos publicly"), so public copy says sharing is the default with an opt-out. If you want sharing to be non-negotiable, the agreement and booking form must change first.
